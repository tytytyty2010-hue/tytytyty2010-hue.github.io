CreatorDesk

CreatorDesk is a private desktop application used by its author to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

CreatorDesk signs in with Google only to read the statistics of the author's own YouTube channels (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API) and, when the author explicitly confirms it, to update the title, description, tags, thumbnail and playlist of his own videos.

  • Data received from Google is stored only on the author's own computer, in an encrypted local database (Windows DPAPI).
  • The data is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party and is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

CreatorDesk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms of Service

CreatorDesk is provided as is, for the personal use of its author only. No service is offered to other users.
